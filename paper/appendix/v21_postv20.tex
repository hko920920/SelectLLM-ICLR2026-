\section{Approximate Transcript Stability}
\label{app:v21-stability}

\begin{proposition}[Approximate transcript stability]
\label{prop:approximate-stability}
Consider two $B$-step root-level experiments with the same initial state and conditional next-state kernels $P_t(\cdot\mid h)$ and $Q_t(\cdot\mid h)$. If
\[
\sup_h \operatorname{TV}\!\left(P_t(\cdot\mid h),Q_t(\cdot\mid h)\right)\leq\varepsilon_t,
\]
then
\[
\operatorname{TV}\!\left(\mathcal L(T_B),\mathcal L(T'_B)\right)
\leq 1-\prod_{t=1}^B(1-\varepsilon_t)
\leq \min\!\left\{1,\sum_{t=1}^B\varepsilon_t\right\}.
\]
For any transcript cost whose range has width at most $M$, the absolute difference in expected cost is at most $M\min\{1,\sum_t\varepsilon_t\}$.
\end{proposition}

Couple the experiments sequentially. Conditional on equal histories through step $t-1$, apply a maximal coupling to the two next-state kernels. Their next states disagree with probability at most $\varepsilon_t$, so the complete histories remain equal with probability at least $\prod_t(1-\varepsilon_t)$. The coupling characterization of total variation gives the first inequality; the union bound gives the second. Translating and rescaling a width-$M$ cost to $[0,1]$ and applying the variational characterization gives the expectation bound. No independence between steps is assumed.
