\section{Proof of Approximate Transcript Stability}
\label{app:v21-stability}

We prove Proposition~\ref{prop:approximate-stability}. Couple the two experiments sequentially. They begin with the same empty history. Conditional on the coupled histories remaining equal through step $t-1$, apply a maximal coupling to $P_t(\cdot\mid h)$ and $Q_t(\cdot\mid h)$. The probability that the next states disagree is at most $\varepsilon_t$. Therefore the probability that the complete histories remain equal through all $B$ steps is at least
\[
\prod_{t=1}^B(1-\varepsilon_t).
\]
The coupling characterization of total variation gives
\[
\operatorname{TV}\!\left(\mathcal L(T_B),\mathcal L(T'_B)\right)
\leq 1-\prod_{t=1}^B(1-\varepsilon_t).
\]
The union bound, equivalently $1-\prod_t(1-\varepsilon_t)\leq\sum_t\varepsilon_t$, yields the second inequality, capped by one.

If $C$ has range contained in an interval of width $M$, translate and rescale it to a function $f\in[0,1]$. The variational characterization of total variation then gives
\[
\left|\mathbb E C(T_B)-\mathbb E C(T'_B)\right|
\leq M\,\operatorname{TV}\!\left(\mathcal L(T_B),\mathcal L(T'_B)\right),
\]
which proves the cost bound. No independence between steps is assumed; the condition is uniform over the common history at which the coupled processes have not yet diverged.
