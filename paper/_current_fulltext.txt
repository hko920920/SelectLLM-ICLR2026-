011                                                                  A BSTRACT
012
013                 Active model selection uses candidate outputs to decide which costly examples to
014                 label, so the supplied list silently defines a prior over the experiment. We show that
015
                    this representation is not invariant. In frozen responses from 31 models, restoring
                    one natural exact version alias changes all 1,500 paired acquisition paths despite
016
                    adding no behavior; registry-independent querying is unchanged. We formalize the
017
                    missing candidate evidence frame and prove a tight annotation-budget-linear lower
018                 bound for selectors that see responses but not which entries deserve independent
019                 mass. Our main causal intervention adds four response-distinct wrappers using
020                 public references. Each exactly preserves its parent’s correctness, yet cumulative
021                 regret rises by 3.25/3.78/.85 on MedQA/GSM8K/OpenBookQA and terminal regret
022                 by 2.91/10.56 points on the first two. Fixed-query effects are zero; 16 candidate
023                 permutations and all nine exact tie policies preserve the result. A reference-free test
024                 passes corrected terminal and active-minus-fixed gates on 3/8 untouched holdouts.
025                 On previously unused AG News, four independently optimized 50,817-parameter
026
                    contextual adapters preserve all parent hard answers without test-reference or item
                    lookup, yet raise sealed terminal regret by .547 [.502, .593] points; active-minus-
027
                    fixed matches and fixed-query effects are zero. An official pairwise-judge selector
028
                    passes on 2/6 datasets. Exact quotienting misses non-identical aliases, prediction-
029                 only defenses trade suppression against clean selection, and untargeted additions
030                 are mostly benign. Together these results establish evidence-frame dependence and
031                 a targeted evaluation-integrity attack surface, not universal instability or production
032                 compromise; active evaluation should make candidate mass auditable.

073   Sequential clone sensitivity is prior: active agent evaluation tests perturbed clones and UNREAL
074   quotients redundant committees; private variants also affect static leaderboards (Lanctot et al., 2026;
075   Nguyen et al., 2025; Singh et al., 2025; Hays et al., 2026). We therefore claim neither the first

      sequential clone effect nor the first multiplicity attack. Our object is a latent same-root refinement

077
      the resulting root-level regret. Static ranks can be recomputed after quotienting, but an acquisition

091
092
            1. We define registry refinement and evidence-frame ambiguity, then show that a natural exact
093            alias rewrites every paired path across three tasks without adding behavior.
094
095         2. Executable, correctness-preserving refinements cause fresh-pool harm. A reference-free
096            extension passes 3/8 untouched holdouts; genuinely learned raw-input adapters pass a sealed

105   We do not claim that every active selector is vulnerable, that a natural alias is adversarial, that every
106   refinement is harmful, or that similarity reveals ownership. The resulting reliability requirement is
107   narrower: an active evaluation should either bind submitted entries to a declared candidate evidence

      Active selection. Let X = {x1 , . . . , xN } be an unlabeled evaluation pool and E = {e1 , . . . , eH }
111   the submitted candidate entries. Entry e exposes response vector ae = (ae (x1 ), . . . , ae (xN )) and
112   has oracle utility vector ue , binary per-example correctness in our experiments. An active policy π
113   sequentially acquires B references. After step t, its transcript contains the queried indices, acquired
114   references, and selected entries; using an oracle root map, we evaluate the corresponding root-level
115   choices rb1 , . . . , rbt .
116   Definition 1 (Candidate evidence frame). A frame Φ = (R, ρ, w) maps submitted entries to ac-
117   countable candidate roots through ρ : E → R and assigns root mass wr . A root is the unit that the
118   evaluation claim intends to count once; it need not be observable or cryptographically authenticated.
119   Two frames are response-compatible when they expose the same entry identifiers and response vectors.
120   Definition 2 (Registry refinement). A refinement of root r adds entries mapped to r without modifying
121   existing entries. It is exact when an added response vector copies its parent and quality-equivalent
122   when its oracle utility vector equals the parent’s coordinate-wise, even if their responses differ.
123   Root-level refinement integrity requires a declared within-root rule under which such representation
124   changes do not silently redefine the evaluation claim.
125
      For example, a uniform per-entry mechanism induces the empirical measure µE = |E|−1 e∈E δae .
                                                                                                  P
126   Adding k entries changes one root’s represented mass without adding a root; candidate-consensus
127   mechanisms can change analogously because consensus is computed over listed entries. A policy is
128   frame-blind when it observes responses and acquired references but not authenticated ρ or w.
