\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Active model selection and evaluation.}
Active Testing, Online Active Model Selection, MODEL SELECTOR, CODA, LLM Selector, and Select-LLM reduce evaluation cost by choosing labels from candidate predictions or judge views \citep{kossen2021activetesting,rezakarimi2021onlineams,okanovic2025modelselector,kay2025coda,durmazkeser2025llmselector,durmazkeser2026selectllm}. Their submitted candidates are normally the counting unit. We audit the antecedent question: whether that list is a valid representation of the accountable alternatives. One shared reference scores every entry, so multiplicity can change both posterior mass and the future evidence set.

\paragraph{Strategic replication and clone robustness.}
Strategic-replication bandits let known agents register duplicate arms and design agent-first or replication-proof mechanisms \citep{shin2022strategicreplication,shin2025replicationproof}. Ownership is supplied and rewards are arm-local; our roots can be latent, while one label updates all entries and the endpoint is selected-root regret. Clone-Robust AI Alignment and metric clone weights instead require static reward learning or aggregation to resist near-duplicate alternatives \citep{procaccia2025clonerobust,berriaud2026clone,berriaud2026consensus}. These works motivate our response-only baselines. They do not recover labels skipped by an already changed acquisition transcript, and similarity alone does not authenticate a root map.

\paragraph{Redundancy in sequential evaluation and active learning.}
Active evaluation studies perturbed agents, while UNREAL and PUTSC quotient redundant committees or candidate predictors \citep{lanctot2026activeevaluation,nguyen2025unreal,liang2020putsc}. This is the closest sequential boundary. UNREAL uses a reduced Rashomon ensemble to train a downstream predictor; we select among submitted predictors and evaluate an oracle root-level decision. More generally, we do not claim the first sequential clone effect. The additional obligation here is the combination of latent same-root refinement, shared-label evidence, an irreversible acquisition transcript, and fixed-query mediation to terminal root cost.

\paragraph{Arenas, leaderboards, false names, and lineage.}
Strategic candidacy and leaderboard audits show that submitted models, match records, preferences, or benchmark subsets can alter a fixed ranking \citep{hays2026strategic,singh2025leaderboard,huang2026dropping,yauney2026microbenchmark}. Our result is complementary: registry representation changes which evidence is collected before ranking. Dilution priors, false-name quotients, and lineage attestation clarify why a counting unit needs information beyond predictions \citep{george2010dilution,burnat2026quotient,shang2026lineage}. We use authenticated lineage as an oracle-information remedy, not as a lineage system contributed by this paper. Thus our novelty is a meaningful sequential extension at the active-evaluation boundary, rather than priority over clones, Sybils, multiplicity, or ranking manipulation in general.
