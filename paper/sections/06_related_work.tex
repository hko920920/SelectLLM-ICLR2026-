\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Closest conceptually, strategic-replication bandits let known agents register duplicate arms and motivate agent-first defenses \citep{shin2022strategicreplication,shin2025replicationproof}. They observe arm-local rewards under supplied ownership; here roots are latent, one label scores every entry, and selection ends at a root. Active evaluation tests separately scored perturbed clones; UNREAL and PUTSC quotient redundant committees or candidates \citep{lanctot2026activeevaluation,nguyen2025unreal,liang2020putsc}. We claim neither the first sequential replication nor clone effect. Our remaining object is shared-label exact-utility refinement with fixed-query mediation to root regret (Table~\ref{tab:closest-boundary}); correlation-aware selection fixes an ensemble \citep{turkmen2026information}.

Static registry attacks alter ranks, models, matches, or descriptions, not future labels \citep{hays2026strategic,suri2025trojanclimb,singh2025leaderboard,huang2026dropping}. Dilution priors, clone weights, quotients, and lineage constrain static influence or identify roots \citep{george2010dilution,berriaud2026consensus,procaccia2025clonerobust,burnat2026quotient,shang2026lineage}; ours changes which reusable labels are acquired, which post-hoc quotienting cannot recover.
