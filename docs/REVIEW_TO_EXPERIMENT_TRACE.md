## Decision rule for future work

Do not generate additional tasks until one passes.  That would convert a
prospective generality question into an unreported search.  Any future study
must disclose Steps 105, 113, and 114, explain why a new task is scientifically
needed, and freeze its roster and gate eligibility without using the unseen
outcome.

## What is already sufficient for the paper

The causal chain is supported for the targeted T2 and held-out IMDB
conditions: refinement changes candidate mass, acquisition paths, acquired
evidence, and downstream root-level cost; fixed-query equality isolates the
mediation.  The open item is breadth and operational transfer, not the internal
validity of that result.
