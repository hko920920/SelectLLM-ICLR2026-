# Step 97 review-closure report

## What was being closed

Two independent reviews converged on one material open bridge: the strongest
terminal result used public/acquired references, whereas the previous learned
single-similarity replication changed paths and cumulative regret but did not
produce material terminal harm.  A second review also identified
strategic-replication bandits as a missing closest conceptual comparison.

## Diagnosis before any new sealed test was opened

The frozen Step 95 verification partition was diagnosed after retaining its
development stop.  Its final-root changes were almost entirely bidirectional
transitions between two roots separated by only about one global accuracy
point.  Conditional on a changed root, the mean loss was about .22 percentage
points; with roughly 19--21% final-root changes this could not reach the
predeclared .5-point terminal gate.  This established a directional terminal
geometry requirement rather than a need for more indiscriminate search.

## Prospective Step 96 SNLI audit

Before inspecting any SNLI row or outcome, Step 96 froze the dataset revision,
four-root registry, budget, partitions, model-to-label mappings, adapter
architecture, 48-cell development grid, top-eight validation rule, and one
sealed official test.  Four independently seeded 14,767,874-parameter error
adapters trained only on raw SNLI train text and labels.  Acquisition and
posterior evidence use the same literal exact-match similarity.  The endpoint
receives no reference, peer output, item lookup, pool, selector state, or test
label at runtime.

The development gate passed.  A pre-test amendment replaced a floating-point
comparison of exactly 45/4500 with its preregistered integer-count meaning; the
original ledgers remain bundled, the selected condition and all effects were
unchanged, and test outcomes were still sealed.  A second amendment fixed the
previously omitted confirmatory random seeds before label-free test predictions
were hashed and before the one-time sealed outcome opening.

On 3,000 locked test pools, terminal and active-minus-fixed harm were both
+1.6305 percentage points with positive 95% lower bounds; cumulative regret was
+.3161, 99.13% of paths changed, parent-to-challenger transitions exceeded the
reverse direction, and fixed-query histories and effects were exactly equal.
The literal primary result nevertheless remains
`NO_GO_RETAIN_STEP96_NEGATIVE`: one alias lost 100/9,824 correct examples, two
above the allowed 98 (1.0179% rather than at most 1%).  An independent validator
reconstructed all bindings, pools, trajectories, effects, inference, gates, and
that exact no-go decision.

After retaining the primary decision, every one of the eight already developed
top conditions was evaluated.  They collapse to two unique threshold vectors.
The conservative .5%-construction threshold loses 31--47/9,824 correct
examples, changes 99.43% of paths, and yields terminal and active-minus-fixed
harm of +.9145 points with positive lower bounds, Holm q=1.99998e-5 across the
two unique conditions, cumulative regret +.2210, and exact fixed-query zero.
This is explicitly `POSTCONFIRMATORY_EXPLORATORY_NOT_PRIMARY_CONFIRMATION`; it
does not replace the selected primary row.

## Prospective Step 97 SciTail audit

Step 97 prospectively fixed a single conservative condition on a new task.  It
stopped before adapter training because the preregistered roster gate failed:
the parent exceeded the best challenger by only .4 points on the roster gate,
below the required 2.5 points.  The sealed SciTail test outcome was not opened,
and an independent validator reconstructed the stop and absence of later
outputs.  No further task shopping was performed.

## Manuscript and novelty closure

The paper now compares directly with strategic-replication bandits and states
the remaining distinctions: known arm ownership versus latent accountable
roots, arm-local rewards versus one reusable label updating all candidates, and
played-arm cumulative reward versus an acquired-label transcript followed by a
terminal root decision.  The abstract, results, limitations, appendix, and
reproducibility statement report both the Step 96 primary no-go and the
secondary sensitivity, plus the Step 97 unopened-test stop.

## Bottom line

The prior scientific bottleneck is no longer an absence of learned,
reference-free, source-faithful terminal evidence: that mechanism is materially
present on SNLI.  The cleanest confirmatory label remains a near-miss because of
two quality-count examples, while the complete predeveloped sensitivity passes
all scientific gates only as secondary evidence.  The remaining limitation is
therefore narrower and accurately bounded: no live registry admission or full
independent base checkpoint is demonstrated, and the secondary row is not a
replacement preregistered confirmation.
