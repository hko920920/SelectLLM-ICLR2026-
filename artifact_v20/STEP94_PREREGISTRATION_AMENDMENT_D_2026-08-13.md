roster members as alias parents but then made confirmatory task eligibility

Stage-A outcome.  The frozen parent set remains the top three members selected
by the Stage-A search partition; no lower-ranked root may be introduced.
Search/verification parent rank and whether it is the best member are still
reported in full.  All quality-loss, path-change, terminal-materiality,
confidence, active-minus-fixed, cumulative, and exact fixed-query gates remain
unchanged.
