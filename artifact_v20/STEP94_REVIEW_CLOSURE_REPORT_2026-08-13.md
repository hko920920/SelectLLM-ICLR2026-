# Step 94 fresh-task learned single-similarity review closure

## Decision-changing condition audited

The remaining high-value reviewer condition was not logically impossible. It
asked whether a previously unused task could show material terminal and
active-minus-fixed harm using learned, raw-input, no-test-reference variants
under a source-faithful selector with the same similarity for acquisition and
posterior evidence, while preserving exact fixed-query equality.

Step 94 tested that condition once under an outcome-blind protocol. It fixed a
finite three-task menu, deterministic development partitions, model families,
search space, verification procedure, task-selection rule, gates, and stopping
rule. Test inputs and outcomes were hash-bound before Stage A. Only the selected
20 Newsgroups outcome was authorized for the confirmatory run; MASSIVE and
SST-5 were not used as replacement tests.

## What passed

- The selected variants are four distinct integrated raw-input checkpoints,
  each with 205,245 learned parameters.
- The endpoint has no test-reference, peer-output, item-lookup, dataset-ID, or
  separate feedback input.
- The same literal exact-match similarity drives candidate agreement and
  reference feedback.
- Parent replay is exact; every alias is coordinate-wise non-improving and
  loses only 0.252--0.491 accuracy points.
- Acquisition-path and query-set change rates are both 100%; the terminal root
  changes in 48.2% of paired runs.
- Cumulative regret rises by 0.082157 [0.038544, 0.126554], with one-sided
  sign-flip p=.00020.
- Fixed-query terminal and cumulative effects are exactly zero, and fixed root
  histories are bitwise identical.
- A separate validator reconstructs hashes, raw deltas, quality constraints,
  inference, mechanisms, gates, and the retained decision.

## What failed

The sealed terminal mean is +0.1198 percentage points, its 95% paired bootstrap
interval is [-0.0444, +0.2895], and its one-sided sign-flip p-value is .0813.
It therefore fails the frozen +0.5-point materiality, positive-lower-bound, and
p<=.05 gates. The active-minus-fixed terminal effect is identical because the
fixed-query effect is zero and fails the corresponding terminal gates as well.

The literal decision is `NO_GO_RETAIN_FRESH_TASK_NEGATIVE`. It is not relabeled
as a success, and no unselected test is opened to search for one.

## Scientific closure

This is not a null exercise. Banking77 and fresh 20 Newsgroups now independently
show that learned, no-lookup, source-faithful single-similarity variants can
change nearly every active path and produce positive cumulative/anytime cost
with exact fixed-query mediation. Step 94 additionally upgrades the executable
object from a separate gate file to an integrated parent-plus-gate checkpoint.

The result does not close the stronger terminal-operational bridge. It cannot
support a claim of material terminal harm from an independently trained full
checkpoint or a realistically admitted production endpoint. The manuscript
states this boundary in the abstract, protocol, results, limitations, appendix,
and evidence table.

## Reviewer-feedback closure

The earlier method-fidelity criticism is closed at the implementation level:
Steps 93--94 use one literal similarity for both selector operations and retain
their negative terminal outcomes. The stronger significance condition remains
empirically open, not structurally impossible. A future attempt must be a new,
prospectively designed study with an independently trained or realistically
admissible variant and a mechanism expected to create a larger stable terminal
root gap; repeating the present task menu until one test passes would be
post-selection and is excluded by the stopping rule.

Numerical evidence changed: yes. Step 94 adds a fresh preregistered result and
does not alter any earlier locked measurement.
