# Step 105 prospective one-shot closure

## Binding decision

`NO_GO_RETAIN_STEP105_PROSPECTIVE_NEGATIVE`

The result is not relabeled, the preregistered +0.5 percentage-point
materiality threshold is not lowered, and no replacement task or second Yelp
outcome is authorized.

## What passed

- The roster, parent, one inherited adapter/threshold recipe, selector setting,
  3,000 seeds, outcome gates, and no-replacement rule were fixed before any
  Yelp row or model-weight execution.
- There was no target-task selector grid, search partition, verification
  partition, geometry screen, or outcome-dependent condition choice.
- Four hash-distinct 14,767,874-parameter raw-text adapters were trained without
  test references, item lookup, peer output, pool, or selector state.
- The same literal exact-match function supplied acquisition and evidence.
- Every alias was coordinate-wise non-improving and lost only 0.376--0.561
  accuracy points, within the frozen one-point cap.
- Ordered paths changed in 99.90% of 3,000 paired pools; query sets changed in
  99.87% and final roots in 41.70%.
- Parent-to-challenger transitions exceeded the reverse, 26.53% versus 9.37%.
- Terminal and active-minus-fixed harm were +0.3527 points. Their paired 95%
  intervals were [+0.3090,+0.3977] and [+0.3076,+0.3956], with one-sided
  sign-flip p=9.9999e-6.
- Cumulative regret rose by +0.017925 [+0.016163,+0.019706].
- Fixed-query root histories and terminal/cumulative effects were exactly equal.
- A runner-independent reconstruction reproduced the arrays, inference, gates,
  and literal decision without importing the experimental runner.

## What failed

Only two of the fifteen scientific gate families failed: mean terminal harm
and mean active-minus-fixed terminal harm did not reach the frozen +0.5-point
materiality threshold. The shortfall was 0.1473 percentage point. Positive
inference cannot substitute for the preregistered effect-size requirement.

## Scientific interpretation

Step 105 is stronger than a null: it prospectively reproduces path change,
directional terminal cost, cumulative cost, and exact acquisition mediation on
a new public checkpoint roster with no target selector tuning. It is weaker
than the requested decisive GO: the effect does not meet the authors' own
materiality threshold. It therefore supports a statistically directional
cross-benchmark near-miss while leaving broad prospective generality open.
Yelp and IMDB are both binary sentiment tasks, so the transfer is across
benchmarks and registries within one task family, not across task families or
into a live registry.
