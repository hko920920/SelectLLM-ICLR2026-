## Stage interpretation

- Stage 0 establishes metadata eligibility only.
- Stage 1 establishes exact artifact, data, partition, and label-seal bindings.
- Stage 2 establishes clean safety execution and deterministic parent selection.
- Stage 3 may establish an executable learned derived-endpoint recipe that
  satisfies safety quality gates.
- The input-only pre-outcome stage may establish primary/deployment trigger and
  manifest eligibility without labels.
- Only the final outcome stage can establish or reject the prospective causal,
  remedy, and deployment claims.

Every STOP, NO-GO, or INVALID receipt remains part of the evidence record.
